# P3: Voice capture & STT

Effort: ~26 h (hours are the estimate; we plan by scope, not dates)

## Goal
Record on our phones, upload safely, and get a transcript from the single winning STT provider.
Uploads are idempotent per uploadId, no audio is written to server disk, and the phone keeps the audio until the transcript is saved.

## Scope
- `POST /transcriptions` with the D-02 adapter, vocabulary biasing, limits, and replay mode
- Next.js web skeleton with rewrites and generated types
- Recorder: state machine, IndexedDB chunks, MIME fallback, waveform, wake lock, 5-minute cap
- Device tests on both phones

## Out of scope
- Talk sheet and tray UI (P4)
- Second STT adapter (V1 hardening)
- Streaming STT (V2)

## Exit gate
Copied exactly from [roadmap-and-testing.md](../../roadmap-and-testing.md) §14 (P3 acceptance):

- Recording works on both our phones, in the browser and as an installed PWA.
- A tab killed mid-upload offers a retry with the audio intact.
- S25 passes.
- Stop → transcript p95 ≤ 5 s on a 60 s clip.
